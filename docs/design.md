# Vox visual direction

The supplied dark dashboard is the visual authority. A quiet municipal analytics workspace surrounds a deliberate focal point: the score trajectory and its relationship to service quality.

- Canvas `#0d1019`; sidebar `#111420`; panels `#171b28`; borders `#2a3040`; text `#f1f3fa`; accent `#a4afff`. Green `#78d8b1`, amber `#edc27c`, rose `#e898a5` encode status with written labels.
- Sora is bundled locally as a variable WOFF2 font for all screens, controls and chart labels, without third-party runtime requests. PDF reports embed the regular TrueType face. The SIL Open Font License is included in public/fonts/OFL-Sora.txt. Numeric metrics use tabular figures, restrained weight and negative tracking.
- A 232px navigation rail, a compact header, shared filters, four core metrics, a wide trend next to priorities, category and volume plots, then a sortable unit table. All cards have contextual headings and sample sizes.
- Rounded surfaces and subtle top lighting match the reference; no decorative images compete with evaluation data. Kiosks use a separate, calm, high-contrast layout with oversized touch targets.
- Desktop aligns content to the left and data columns to the right. On mobile, the rail becomes an accessible drawer and analytical sections stack.

Review against the brief: the dark card treatment is intentional because the reference requires it. Replaced generic revenue and customer widgets with the exact six healthcare criteria, transparent scores, sample warnings and operational priorities. Demonstration data is always explicitly labeled.
